Drone Show FAQs

How much does a drone light show cost in India?

The cost depends on the number of drones, the length of the show and the venue location. A compact show uses fewer drones and a shorter sequence, while large festival and government shows use hundreds of drones with fully custom formations. Share your date, venue and audience size with us for an exact quote.

How many drones are needed for a wedding drone show?

Most wedding shows use between 50 and 200 drones. More drones mean larger, more detailed formations — names, portraits, rings — that stay clearly readable from the ground across a bigger venue.

Do you need government permission for a drone show?

Yes. Drone light shows in India need clearances under DGCA rules along with local police and venue permissions. LIGHTINGSKY handles the permission process as part of the booking.

How far in advance should a drone show be booked?

Book at least three to four weeks ahead. Custom formation design, permissions and a site survey all take time, and wedding and festival dates fill up quickly.

Can the formations be customised to our logo or names?

Yes. Every show is designed from scratch, so company logos, couple names, Gujarati and Hindi text, countdowns and product shapes can all be built into the formations.

Which cities does LIGHTINGSKY cover?

We are based in Nikol, Ahmedabad and perform drone light shows across Gujarat and the rest of India, including Surat, Vadodara, Rajkot and destination wedding venues.

Are drone light shows safe?

Yes. The drones fly a pre-programmed, collision-checked path at a safe distance from the audience, with no fire, smoke or falling debris — a quieter and cleaner alternative to fireworks.
